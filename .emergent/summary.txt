<product_requirements>
The WeFix.lk app offers electronics repair and parts sales for Android in Sri Lanka. It enables TV repair bookings (brand, model, issue, address), real-time repair tracking, and warranty checks (serial, bill, phone). The app supports product sales (currently mock data/AsyncStorage) and leverages Firebase for authentication, data storage (Firestore, though AsyncStorage is often used for demo), and FCM for notifications. User authentication is email/password, with Google/phone auth deferred. An Admin Dashboard is planned for staff to manage bookings and inventory. Payments are Cash on Delivery (COD) for the MVP. The UI targets a clean, modern design with blue, white, and green colors, uses LKR currency, and is fully responsive. Core authentication, navigation, booking submission, repair tracking (with cancel/delete), warranty checks, new service requests, and a comprehensive Admin Panel (login, dashboard, booking management) are implemented or actively in progress.
</product_requirements>
